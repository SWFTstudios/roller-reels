Motorcycle & car videography across New Jersey and New York City

Roller Reels creates cinematic rolling shots (moving on the road/highway) and static shoots (stationary at a location) for motorcycles and cars. We also build custom route and deliverables packages tailored to your vision.

Rolling shots

Photo and/or video while moving on the road/highway, including cinematic rider coverage.

Static shoots

Photo and/or video while stationary at a location—ideal for controlled lighting and clean compositions.

How it works

  1. Choose rolling or static coverage and select your NJ/NYC route (or custom location).
  2. Confirm your date/time in Cal.com with a $100 deposit.
  3. Receive your deliverables—photos, video, and optional add-ons—based on your package.
