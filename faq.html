GOT QUESTIONS?

Common questions before you book

What's the difference between rolling shots and static shoots?

Rolling shots are captured while you’re moving on the road/highway for dynamic, cinematic coverage. Static shoots are captured while stationary at a location for cleaner compositions and controlled setups.

What deliverables do I get?

Your core deliverables match your package: Photo Only, Video Only, or Both Photo & Video. Optional add-ons (raw footage archive, rush delivery, posters, or a calendar) are selected during booking.

How does the $100 deposit work?

A $100 deposit is required to confirm your booking. It helps lock in your date/time in our schedule; the remaining balance is due based on your selected shoot details.

Do you film in New Jersey and New York City?

Yes. Roller Reels films motorcycle and car videography across New Jersey and New York City, including popular routes and custom locations.

Where do we meet before the shoot?

Most shoots meet at The Home Depot (Holland Tunnel) — 180 Twelfth St, Jersey City, NJ 07310. Use the main parking deck and meet on the Marin Blvd side. We stage there briefly before rolling to the route. Open in Google Maps. Custom or south/west NJ routes may use an alternate meetup — we’ll confirm before shoot day.

How should I prepare for my shoot?

Come ready with your bike/car, your rider lineup, and any references or must-have moments. Arrive at the meetup spot 10 minutes early. If you chose a custom location, share clear details so we can plan efficiently.
